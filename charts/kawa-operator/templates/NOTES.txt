The kawa operator is installed. Check that it reached the gateway:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs deploy/{{ include "kawa-operator.fullname" . }} | grep -i gateway

Then apply a VirtualTopic, Client, Role or Group and read its Ready condition:

  kubectl get virtualtopics.kawa-platform.github.io,clients.kawa-platform.github.io,roles.kawa-platform.github.io,groups.kawa-platform.github.io -A
